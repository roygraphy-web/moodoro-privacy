Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

Moodoro ("the App") is a Pomodoro timer and ambient BGM app. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. We do not use analytics, crash reporting services, or advertising SDKs.

Push Notifications

The App uses local push notifications to alert you when a timer session ends. Notification permission is optional. No notification data is sent to any server.

Audio

All BGM audio files are bundled within the App and played locally on your device. No audio data is collected or transmitted.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

If you have any questions, please contact us at: contact@moodoro.app
